## Release 0.0.1 — Project Merlin is live

The first public release of Project Merlin: data and research for small yet fierce progressive organizations.

### New

- **Landing site.** A new home for Project Merlin with an overview of the platform and quick links to the data platform and research work.
- **Feature highlights.** Top-level summaries of the three pillars of the platform:
  - **Data Engine** — ingest, clean, and transform geospatial data from NGPVan, NYC Open Data, custom voter files, and more.
  - **Mapping and GIS** — draw turf lines, identify key districts, and surface insights from geospatial data.
  - **Research & Analysis Models** — predictive models and analysis pipelines built to support decision-making.
- **Sticky navbar.** A persistent top navigation bar with quick access to Data Platform and Research. The bar hides on scroll-down and reappears on scroll-up to stay out of your way.
- **Animated wordmark.** The "Project Merlin" headline now uses a scramble-in animation on load for a more polished first impression.
- **Light and dark themes.** A refreshed color palette that renders cleanly in both light and dark mode.

### Docs

- Launched the [documentation site](/) with sections covering the app shell, components, and shared utilities.
