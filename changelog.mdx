<Update label="May 24, 2026">

## New features

**Accounts and authentication**

You can now sign up, sign in, and manage your account directly from the web app. New sign-in and sign-up pages are available, and the navbar shows your account menu once you're signed in.

- Visit `/sign-up` to create an account.
- Visit `/sign-in` to log in to an existing account.
- Use the account menu in the navbar to manage your profile or sign out.

All app pages now require an account, so make sure to sign in before navigating to protected areas.

</Update>
